Before investing a single pound in marketing, ensure your foundation is solid. Traffic is worthless if your store can't convert visitors into customers.

### Store Health Check

Your marketing efforts will be wasted if your store has underlying issues. Our Shopify Site Audit identifies problems that kill conversions:

- **Security vulnerabilities** that erode customer trust

We've seen stores increase conversions by 30% simply by fixing technical issues uncovered in our audit. Don't build a leaky bucket—plug the holes before pouring in traffic.

### SMS Marketing for Immediate Impact

SMS boasts open rates exceeding 98%, making it unmatched for time-sensitive promotions. We help you use SMS strategically:

## Putting It All Together: Your Marketing Strategy

Effective marketing isn't about choosing one channel. It's about orchestrating multiple channels into a cohesive strategy.

### The Customer Journey

Different marketing channels serve different purposes in the customer journey:

1. **Awareness** – Social media ads, content marketing, AI SEO
2. **Consideration** – Email nurturing, retargeting ads, social proof
3. **Conversion** – Optimised landing pages, abandoned cart recovery, compelling offers
4. **Retention** - Post-purchase emails, loyalty programs, exceptional customer service

Your strategy should address each stage systematically.

### Budget Allocation

There's no perfect formula for budget allocation, but consider these principles:

- **Diversify** – Don't rely on a single channel
- **Test small** – Experiment with new channels with minimal investment before scaling
- **Double down on winners** – When something works, invest more
- **Cut losses quickly** – When something doesn't work, move on
